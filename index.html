PERSONAL AUTOMATION PROJECT

Momentum

Momentum is a personal automation project designed to organize and process digital content through automated workflows and connected services.

What Momentum does

Momentum can use authorized Google services to store generated files in Google Drive and maintain workflow records in Google Sheets. Access is used only for the functionality configured by the owner.

Data and access

Momentum does not sell personal information. Connected Google data is accessed only as needed to perform the configured automation. OAuth credentials and tokens are kept in protected configuration and are not published in this repository.
