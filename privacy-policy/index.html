Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Momentum, a personal automation project, handles information when it uses connected Google services.

Information accessed

When authorized by the owner, Momentum may access Google Drive and Google Sheets data required for its configured automation. This can include file metadata, files created by the automation, and spreadsheet records used to track workflow status.

How information is used

Information is used only to operate the configured automation, including creating or locating generated files in Google Drive and reading or updating workflow records in Google Sheets.

Sharing

Momentum does not sell personal information. Google data is not shared with third parties except where necessary to use the connected Google services themselves or where required by law.

Data retention and deletion

Data remains in the connected Google account according to the owner's Google Drive and Google Sheets settings. The owner can delete files, spreadsheet records, or revoke Momentum's Google access at any time through the relevant Google account controls.

Security

OAuth credentials and tokens used by the automation are stored in protected configuration and are not intentionally published in the public repository. No security system can guarantee absolute protection, but reasonable measures are used to protect credentials and workflow data.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the project's data practices change.

Contact

For questions about this policy or Momentum, use the contact information associated with the project.
